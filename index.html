Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does in-home tutoring work with HomeTutor Academy?

After selecting a tutor and completing a booking with your location details, your chosen tutor visits your home at the agreed date and time to provide 1-on-1 private instruction.

Are the tutors background-checked and verified?

Yes! All tutors undergo identity verification, degree check, and profile review before being published on our marketplace.

Which areas in Addis Ababa do you cover?

We cover key sub-cities across Addis Ababa including Bole, Kazanchis, Old Airport, CMC, Megenagna, Sarbet, Nifas Silk, and surrounding areas.

How are payments processed?

Bookings are safely processed via integrated Ethiopian payment options like Chapa. You can reserve individual sessions or recurring weekly blocks.
