Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a tutor?

Browse available tutors, select the tutor you want, click "Book Lesson", choose your lesson details, and complete the booking process.

Are tutors verified?

Tutor profiles may go through an approval process before becoming publicly visible on the platform.

What subjects are available?

Subjects may include Mathematics, Chemistry, Biology, Physics, History, Geography, Economics, and other subjects offered by registered tutors.

How are payments handled?

Your booking page can use an integrated Ethiopian payment provider such as Chapa. The final payment system should use secure server-side verification before production launch.

Can I become a tutor?

Yes. Click "Become a Tutor", submit your information, and your application can be reviewed before publication.
