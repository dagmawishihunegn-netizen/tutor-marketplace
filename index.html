Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How does in-home tutoring work with HomeTutor Academy?

After selecting a tutor and completing a booking with your location details, your chosen tutor visits your home at the agreed date and time to deliver 1-on-1 private academic instruction.

Are the tutors background-checked and verified?

Yes! All tutors undergo identity verification, degree verification, and background evaluation before being listed on our platform.

Which sub-cities in Addis Ababa do you cover?

We serve all sub-cities across Addis Ababa including Bole, Yeka, Kirkos, Nifas Silk, Arada, Gullele, Lideta, Akaki Kality, Kolfe Keraniyo, and Addis Ketema.

How are payments processed?

Bookings are securely processed via integrated Ethiopian payment gateways like Chapa. You can reserve individual sessions or recurring weekly blocks.
